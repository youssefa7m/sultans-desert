Our Story

More Than a Trip, It's an Experience

Welcome to Sultans Desert, your gateway to discovering the beauty, culture, and adventure of Morocco. We help visitors explore the landscapes around Marrakech and the Agafay Desert through memorable local experiences.

From exciting quad and buggy rides to traditional camel rides, Moroccan food, and beautiful desert evenings, there is something for every kind of traveller.

Our aim is to make your experience enjoyable, memorable, and easy to plan, while sharing the welcoming spirit of Morocco. We look forward to welcoming you.
