WELCOME TO SULTANS DESERT

More Than a Trip.
A Moroccan Experience.

Welcome to Sultans Desert, your gateway to unforgettable adventures and authentic Moroccan experiences in Marrakech and the breathtaking Agafay Desert.

Our mission is to help you discover the beauty, culture and adventure of Morocco through carefully selected experiences designed to create lasting memories.

From thrilling quad biking and buggy adventures across desert trails to traditional camel rides, authentic Moroccan cuisine, relaxing pool experiences and magical evenings under the desert sky, we bring together memorable experiences in Marrakech and Agafay.

At Sultans Desert, we believe every journey should be more than just a visit. It should be an opportunity to discover new landscapes, experience local traditions and enjoy genuine Moroccan hospitality.

We work with local activity providers and experienced guides to coordinate experiences, assist with arrangements and make planning your adventure as simple as possible.

Whether you are travelling as a couple, with friends or with family, we aim to help you find the right experience for your stay in Morocco.
